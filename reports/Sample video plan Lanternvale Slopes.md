## Promise–payoff audit

1. **The literal promise:** someone spent [N] weekends on a 40-second snowboarding game, and I'll see what's in those 40 seconds and whether it was worth it.
2. **Where it's delivered:** beats 1 to 5 show what's in it; the full run at 7:05 delivers the 40 seconds.
3. **Is the payoff good enough?** Only if the final run is a clean trick run at night. A plain cruise to the lodge would get a "that's it?". If you can't land the goal, end on your best real run and say so. Don't stitch two runs together.
4. **Does the thumbnail image appear?** A is the first shot and is in the final run, B is in beat 1, C is in beat 3.
5. **Are the claims real?** They will be if [N], the bail count and the attempt counter are your true numbers. The bot test gives 39 to 41 seconds for a run, so "about 40" holds.
6. **Is anything withheld too long?** The snow trick is teased at 0:29 and answered by 1:35. The full run waits until the end, but the viewer sees a new section every minute on the way, so the wait is for the assembled run, not for a hidden answer.
